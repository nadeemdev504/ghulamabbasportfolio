About the doctor

A career built on patient care and teaching

Prof. Dr. Ghulam Abbas is Head of the Department of Nephrology at Nishtar Medical University & Hospital Multan. He leads a kidney care programme that joins evidence-based treatment with clear, respectful communication.

Every treatment plan is explained in plain language before it begins, with family members included.

Treatments

Complete kidney care under one roof

Chronic Kidney Disease

Early detection, diet guidance and medicines that slow kidney damage.

Kidney Transplant

Donor assessment, surgery coordination and lifelong follow-up.

Hemodialysis Management

Includes setting dialysis frequency, monitoring fluid removal targets, and adjusting treatments to minimize side effects.

Hypertension and Glomerular Disease

Treatment of kidney-related blood pressure, protein in urine and nephritis.

Acute Kidney Injury

Rapid assessment when kidney function drops suddenly.

Peritoneal Dialysis

Oversees the entire medical strategy, safety, and prescription for peritoneal dialysis (PD)..

Diabetic Kidney Disease

Comprehensive management to prevent diabetic nephropathy, control blood sugar impact, and preserve kidney function.
